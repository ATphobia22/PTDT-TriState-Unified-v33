# IDNR floodway permit + FEMA LOMA (operator checklist)

## IDNR Construction in a Floodway — IC 14-28-1

**Trigger:** erect/maintain structure, obstruction, deposit, or excavation **in a floodway** on a stream with drainage area **> 1 sq mi** (unless exemption/general license).

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Statute | IC 14-28-1; rules 312 IAC 10 |
| Form | State Form **42946** (Permit Application) |
| Fee | Minimum **$200** nonrefundable (typical private applicant) |
| Contact | water_inquiry@dnr.IN.gov · 317-232-4160 / 877-928-3755 |
| Online / paper | https://www.in.gov/dnr/water/regulatory-permit-programs/ |

**Director issues only if** applicant proves project will **not**:
- adversely affect floodway efficiency/capacity (**≥0.15 ft** regulatory flood rise — 312 IAC 10-2-3),
- unduly restrict capacity,
- or create unreasonable hazard to life/property (and related statutory tests).

**Technical packets (examples):** Effective Cross-Sectional Flow (Worksheet A), Bridges/Culverts (Worksheet B), Fish/Wildlife/Botanical (SF 57132), habitat mitigation guidelines.

**General licenses / exemptions** exist (e.g. some streambank protection notifications SF 57632, logjams, certain utility crossings) — still check local FPA + USACE/IDEM.

**Local gate:** If site is regulatory **floodway**, local FPA typically **will not** issue local permit until DNR approval (or documented exemption).

**Fringe:** often local floodplain ordinance only (elevate/openings); DNR floodway permit not always required unless near public freshwater lake/dam setbacks.

## FARA (Indiana) before LOMA / local permit

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| Zone A on FIRM | FARA required for BFE + floodway limits |
| BAFL shows hazard not on FIRM | FARA / best available |
| Drainage area > 1 sq mi unmapped | FARA |

Generate via **INFIP** e-FARA or paper FARA request. DoWORC for prior Division of Water file history.

## FEMA LOMA (natural grade — 44 CFR Part 70 / MT-1)

**LOMA** removes structure/lot from SFHA when **natural grade** LAG (or LLE for lot) is **at or above BFE**. **No fill** (fill path is LOMR-F, fee applies).

| Form | Use |
|---|---|
| **MT-EZ** | Single residential lot/structure |
| **MT-1** / Online LOMC | Multiple lots/structures; LOMR-F |

**Typical package:**
1. Certified elevations (Licensed Surveyor or PE) — Elevation Certificate or elevation form  
2. FIRMette / effective panel  
3. Deed **or** plat + tax assessor map  
4. Community acknowledgment as required by form  

**Fee:** LOMA review **no FEMA fee**; survey cost is owner’s. LOMR-F has FEMA fee.

**PTDT Material Truth (internal, not a filed LOMA):** BFE **375.0 ft** NAVD88, LAG **377.2 ft** → **+2.2 ft** natural clearance **if** survey confirms. **Dual APN / dual lat-lon must be reconciled on deed before filing.**

**Insurance vs state:** LOMA affects **mandatory insurance / SFHA** mapping; state floodway permitting still uses IC 14-28-1 + BAFL/FARA where applicable.
